Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before making a decision.

Which machine is right for my production line?

It depends on your line speed, packaging type, and print content. Our experts will assess your line free of charge and recommend the best fit — contact us for a site visit.

Do you offer installation and training?

Yes, every machine comes with on-site installation, operator training, and a comprehensive AMC option so your team is fully confident from day one.

What surfaces can these printers mark on?

Our CIJ printers cover glass, PET, HDPE, aluminium, foil, cartons, and more. Laser systems work on metals, plastics, ceramics, and glass without any surface prep.

How is after-sales support provided?

We offer 24/7 remote diagnostics, a pan-India service network, and a guaranteed 4-hour response SLA for critical downtime calls. Spare parts are stocked locally.

Can I print QR codes and barcodes?

Absolutely. All our systems support 1D barcodes, QR codes, Data Matrix, logos, and variable data including serial numbers and batch codes.

Do you offer EMI / financing options?

Yes, we partner with leading NBFCs to offer easy EMI schemes so you can start production without a large upfront investment. Ask our team for details.
